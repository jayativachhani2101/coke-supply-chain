Operational Stages
5 Phases
1

Raw Materials Sourcing

Procuring pristine ingredients and high-grade packaging inputs from globally verified partners.

  • Water Purifying: Primary ingredient (~90%) filtered locally in municipal zones.
  • Sweeteners: Agricultural sourcing of sugarcane (tropics) and corn (HFCS in US).
  • Secret Blends: Authorized leaf extract processing via licensed DEA facilities (Stepan NJ).
  • Packaging: High-sustainability PET polymer, structural aluminum, and green glass.
2

Manufacturing & Concentrate

Formulating Merchandise 7X secret syrup at highly controlled export concentrate sites.

  • Secure Plants: Atlanta and Ireland blend secret ingredients in tight secrecy.
  • Decentralized Export: Shipped globally to a network of ~900 franchise bottlers.
  • Carbon Reduction: Light concentrate shipping avoids transporting heavy water bulk.
  • Flavor Stewardship: Strict licensing checks ensure identical, refreshing global taste.
3

The Bottling Partner Model

Leveraging an asset-light franchise model where independent partners localise the brand.

  • Decoupled Operations: The Coca-Cola Company sells concentrate; bottlers operate production.
  • Local Composition: Bottlers blend local water, carbonation CO2, and sweeteners.
  • Regional Dominance: Major partners include Coca-Cola FEMSA, HBC, and CCEP.
  • Capital Efficiency: Keeps corporate costs low while driving massive local job creation.
4

Distribution & Logistics

Omnichannel delivery routing finished products to millions of retail shelves daily.

  • Shelf Supply: Mix of Direct Store Delivery (DSD) and automated depot routes.
  • Smart Fleet: Routing algorithms and electric trucks cut diesel greenhouse output.
  • Cold-Chain Systems: Thermally controlled logistics maintain carbonation stability.
  • Global Presence: Active in 200+ countries, linking megacities and micro-retailers.
5

Sustainability & Circularity

Targeting zero-waste, net-zero greenhouse plants, and water replenishment goals.

  • World Without Waste: Commitment to collect & recycle 100% of packaging by 2030.
  • Food-grade rPET: Used plastic sorted, washed, pelletized, and blown back to bottles.
  • Water Balancing: Replenishing 100%+ of manufacturing water to local aquifers.
  • Scope 3 Cuts: Supplier environmental audits and shared transport cuts chain emissions.
