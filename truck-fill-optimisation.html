Stakeholder Personas

Who Uses TruckFill?

Three distinct user personas interact with the engine at different cadences, each with aligned but differentiated objectives.

Bottler
Weekly Cadence
  • Submit weekly order volumes and SKU breakdowns
  • Maximise order fulfilment accuracy across product lines
  • Accept/reject engine-proposed SKU adjustments per delivery window
  • Visibility into container allocation for their orders
Coke Planner
Daily Cadence
  • Optimise aggregate fill rates across all dispatches
  • Manage SKU hold/pull decisions across weekly boundaries
  • Balance demand signals against container capacity constraints
  • Monitor fill rate KPIs and exception alerts in real-time
Transport Planner
Daily Cadence
  • Minimise total logistics cost per dispatched load
  • Ensure weight/axle compliance for all outbound trucks
  • Coordinate route sequencing with optimised container loads
  • Reduce empty-run ratios and consolidate shipment legs
